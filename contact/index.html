What to tell the dispatcher

Say where you are, what is happening and whether water is still flowing. If you can reach the main valve, tell us whether it closes. Mention any sewage, any smell of gas and any electrical outlets near the water.

If you are on South Hill or outside the city limits, give the street and the nearest cross street, because some addresses share the Puyallup mailing name with unincorporated areas.

Before you call

  • !Close the main valve. If water is flowing, stop it first.
  • !Leave if you smell gas. Call the gas utility or 911 before calling us.
  • !Note what you see. Where the water is coming from helps the plumber bring the right parts.
  • !Have your address ready. Include the cross street if you are on South Hill.
