When a burst pipe is an emergency: the first five minutes

Every minute of flow adds to the damage, so the sequence below matters. Find the main shutoff, close it, open the lowest faucet to drain the lines, and then call.

Know where your main valve is before you need it. In many homes it sits near the water meter, on the street side of a garage wall, or in a basement or crawlspace near where the line enters.

Do this in order

  • !Close the main valve. Turn it clockwise. A ball valve with a lever turns a quarter turn. If it will not move, call us right away.
  • !Drain the lines. Open the lowest faucet and flush a toilet to relieve pressure.
  • !Protect the electrical system. Switch off power to wet areas at the breaker if you can do so dry. Never touch an outlet or panel while standing in water.
  • !Lift valuables and document. Move items off the floor and take photographs for your insurer before cleaning up.

What causes pipes to burst or crack

Freezing is the textbook cause. Water expands as it turns to ice, and the expansion splits copper or plastic at weak points. In Puyallup, cool, wet winters mean freeze events are not constant, but a cold snap can still catch exposed pipes in a crawlspace, an exterior wall or an unheated garage.

Other causes are age and corrosion in galvanized or older copper, high water pressure, a failed fitting, a nail or screw through a pipe, and ground movement that shears a buried line.

A pipe that has burst once is telling you something about the rest of the line. If the pipe is old galvanized or has multiple pinholes, a section repair is a short-term fix, and repiping may cost less than repeated repairs.

Drain pipe repair and cracked pipe

Not every burst is a supply pipe. A cracked drain pipe leaks wastewater instead of clean water, usually after a shift in the ground, an impact or years of corrosion. It may show as a wet spot, a smell, or a dripping ceiling below a bathroom.

We repair drain pipe with sections of new pipe and no-hub or solvent-weld couplings, and for buried lines we use a camera first, as described on sewer inspection.

Frozen pipes before they burst

If a faucet runs slowly or not at all in cold weather, a pipe may be frozen but not yet split. Open the faucet, warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels starting from the faucet end, and never use an open flame. If you see a bulge, frost on the pipe or water, call us.

Shutoffs and cold-season prep in Puyallup

Puyallup sits in a region with cool, wet winters, and a hard freeze is less common than in colder states but it does occur. Insulating exposed pipes in crawlspaces and garages, disconnecting hoses from outdoor spigots and knowing the location of the main shutoff are all inexpensive steps.

If you are in a home served by the City of Puyallup, the city can tell you where your meter shutoff sits, but you should also find your own main valve inside the house.
