Hydro Jetting Plumbing Cost in Puyallup, WA: What Drives the Price

Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure stream of water to scour the inside of a drain or sewer pipe. It is the tool plumbers reach for when grease, scale or roots keep coming back after a cable has opened the line.

People searching for the cost of jetting usually want a number. A responsible answer starts with what moves the number, because two jobs that look alike from the kitchen can be very different underground. This guide lays out the factors, what a quote should include and how to decide between jetting and cabling.

Written by the Puyallup Express Plumbing team · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A drain cable, also called a snake, punches a hole through a blockage. It restores flow quickly, but it leaves a layer of grease and scale on the pipe wall, and the line often narrows again in a few months.

A jetter pushes a nozzle down the pipe and fires water backward and sideways at high pressure. The stream cuts through grease, scrapes scale and flushes loosened debris toward the main. The pipe ends up close to its full diameter, which is why jetting suits restaurants, older cast iron and lines with repeat clogs.

Jetting is a good fit for

  • Repeat clogs in the same line
  • Heavy grease in kitchen drains
  • Scale in older cast iron
  • Roots at joints, after a camera check
  • Preparing a pipe for lining

A cable is usually enough for

  • A first-time clog in a fixture trap
  • A single toilet or sink blockage
  • Hair in a tub drain
  • A line with a visible offset or break, where jetting could do harm

Be wary of one flat price that ignores these factors. A quote that names the line being cleaned, the access point and whether the camera is included is easier to compare.

When jetting is the wrong tool

A jetter is powerful. In a pipe that is cracked, collapsed or held together by corrosion, high pressure can widen the damage. That is why a camera pass often comes first on an older line.

If the camera shows a break, an offset joint or a sag that holds water, the answer is repair or lining, not more water. A good plumber will tell you that even when jetting would have been an easy sale.

Does a jetting visit last

Results depend on the cause. If grease was the problem and you change what goes down the drain, a jetted line can stay clear for years. If roots were the cause, they will grow back unless the entry point is repaired or lined, so jetting is a maintenance step for rooted lines.

For restaurants and shops, many owners put jetting on a schedule rather than waiting for a backup. A planned visit on a quiet morning costs less than an emergency on a Friday night.

Do not try this yourself

  • !Rental jetters can injure. High-pressure water can cut skin, and a hose that whips back is dangerous.
  • !Chemical drain cleaners do not help. They can sit in a blocked pipe and make a later repair unsafe.
  • !Do not jet a line you have not seen. A camera first tells you whether the pipe can take it.

What to do when a drain backs up

  • !Stop using water. Every fixture on the line feeds the blockage.
