What South Hill is, and how it relates to the city

South Hill is a census-designated place just south of Puyallup, set on the plateau above the south side of the Puyallup River valley. The 2020 census counted 64,708 residents there, compared with 42,973 in the City of Puyallup itself. Its addresses use Puyallup as the postal city, with ZIP codes 98373, 98374 and 98375.

South Hill is mostly unincorporated, which means much of it is governed by Pierce County, not by the City of Puyallup. Voters turned down a 1998 proposal to make it a separate city. The City of Puyallup's own comprehensive plan still carries a South Hill Neighborhood Plan, so some South Hill ground sits inside city limits.

Because the line between city and county is not obvious from a mailing address, we describe South Hill by its anchor streets. S Meridian (SR 161) is the main north-south corridor, and Shaw Road links the northern part of the hill to downtown. We do not claim exact boundaries, and we ask for your cross street so the right permit office is clear.

A mix of housing from several eras

Settlement on the hill began in the 1880s, with farms and logging camps. Growth stayed slow until after World War II, when better roads made commuting practical. The state's historic preservation plan notes that development spread beyond downtown toward South Hill between 1950 and 1970. The 2020 census counted 22,634 housing units.

That means a single zip code can hold a 1960s rambler, a cul-de-sac built in the 1990s and a new home finished last year. We do not guess what is inside your walls from the year on the deed. A camera on the drain line and a look at the water heater tell us far more than any date.

Water, sewer and permits depend on your address

Not every South Hill home is on the same water system. Pierce County's review of the Sunrise community, for example, lists Tacoma Water as that area's provider, while homes inside the city use the City of Puyallup system. The city's 2024 water quality report gives an average hardness of 89 ppm for its own supply, and that figure applies to city water customers only.

The same split applies to permits. Inside city limits, plumbing permits go through the City of Puyallup's permit portal. Outside them, Pierce County handles them. Water heater replacement and other permitted plumbing work in Washington follows the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments, so ask the right office before work starts.

South Hill facts worth knowing

South Hill covers about 18 square miles. Nearby Edgewood and Milton are informally called North Hill, and the Puyallup River valley separates the two hills. For sewer and water questions, your own utility bill names the provider.

We also serve nearby areas such as the Sunrise community, downtown Puyallup and Sumner.
