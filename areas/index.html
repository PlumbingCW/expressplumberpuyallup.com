Where Puyallup Express Plumbing dispatches

Dispatch is based in Puyallup and the 24/7 line covers the city, South Hill and the surrounding Pierce County towns. We serve all five Puyallup ZIP codes, 98371 through 98375, which take in downtown, the fairgrounds area, the Meridian corridor and South Hill.

Neighborhoods in and around Puyallup

Three local pages describe areas inside and next to the city. South Hill explains how city and county addresses differ on the plateau. Downtown Puyallup covers the older blocks around Pioneer Park. Sunrise covers the master-planned community in unincorporated Pierce County.

Sumner

Sumner is northeast of Puyallup in the river valley, with the Sounder station, older downtown blocks and a large commercial base. Its page covers flood-season prep, the Sumner ZIP codes 98390 and 98352, and the services we send there most.

Other towns we serve

We also travel to Edgewood and Milton, which people on South Hill sometimes call North Hill, and to Orting, Graham, Spanaway and Bonney Lake. These towns do not have their own pages yet. Call the 24/7 line, tell the dispatcher your address and the problem, and a plumber will be sent.

Each of these places has its own utility, permit office and water provider. We recommend asking your city or district about permits and your meter, and we will explain how it affects the job.

What to expect when you call

The dispatcher asks for your address, what is happening and whether you can reach your main valve. The plumber arrives with a camera, a cable machine and common parts on the truck, explains the scope and the price, and starts only after you agree.

Codes and references for this page

Permit rules depend on the jurisdiction. The City of Puyallup, the City of Sumner and Pierce County each handle their own addresses, and all plumbing work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as amended by Washington.

Washington requires a licensed plumbing contractor for plumbing work done for others under RCW 18.106, and the Department of Labor and Industries lets you check a license at lni.wa.gov/verify.
