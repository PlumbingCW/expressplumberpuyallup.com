What Sunrise is and where it sits

Sunrise is a master-planned community in the South Hill area, in unincorporated Pierce County rather than inside the City of Puyallup. County documents place it about 4 miles south of SR 512 and one mile east of SR 161, on roughly 1,467 acres. A county staff review lists 5,201 approved units, and the plan traces back to an earlier application from 1983.

Builders have worked through it in phases, and public listings show both single-family communities and multifamily projects. We describe Sunrise by those anchors and do not claim a precise boundary, since the plan has been amended several times.

Newer homes have their own plumbing questions

Newer construction usually means plastic or copper supply lines, a builder-grade water heater and standard fixtures. It does not mean problems are absent. Common calls in newer subdivisions include slow drains from hair or grease, a failed fill valve, a leaking supply line to a toilet or faucet and a water heater that has quit.

If your home is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, check the terms before you hire anyone. Some warranties require repairs by an approved contractor, and a call to the builder can save money.

Water, sewer and permits in Sunrise

Pierce County's review of the community lists Tacoma Water as the water provider, supplying Sunrise through a series of mains and tanks. We did not find a sewer provider named in that document, so check your bill if you need to know. Because Sunrise is outside city limits, plumbing permits generally go through Pierce County.

Water pressure matters in a hillside community, so a pressure gauge and a look at the pressure reducing valve are part of many visits.

Codes and references for this page

Plumbing work in Sunrise follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as amended by Washington. Sunrise lies in unincorporated Pierce County, so ask the county permit office which permits apply.

Washington requires a licensed plumbing contractor for plumbing work done for others under RCW 18.106, and the Department of Labor and Industries lets you check a license at lni.wa.gov/verify.
