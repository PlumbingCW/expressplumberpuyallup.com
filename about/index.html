Who we are

Puyallup Express Plumbing is a Puyallup-based plumbing dispatch that answers a 24/7 emergency line and sends licensed plumbers to homes and businesses in Puyallup, South Hill and nearby Pierce County towns. We handle drains, sewers, leaks, water heaters, toilets, fixtures, disposals, repiping and commercial calls.

Our address is 4423 S Meridian, Puyallup, WA 98373, on the S Meridian (SR-161) corridor that runs through South Hill. The line answers at (253) 275-5116 every day of the year.

How to check any plumber in Washington

You do not have to take our word for anything. Washington's Department of Labor and Industries runs a free Verify a Contractor tool at lni.wa.gov/verify. Search by business name, license number or UBI number to see whether a plumbing contractor's license is active, whether its workers' comp account is current, and whether there are citations or bond claims.

Since July 1, 2021, plumbing contract work in Washington must be done by a licensed plumbing contractor under chapter 18.106 RCW, with a certified plumber designated for the business. Use the same tool for anyone you hire.

What we do not do

We do not pump or repair septic tanks, build pool shells or dry out water-damaged buildings. Those are separate trades, and we will say so on the phone so you can call the right company. We also do not give a flat price for jobs we have not seen, and we will not promise an arrival time we cannot keep.

What we handle on a typical week

Most calls fall into a few groups. Drains and sewers come first, from a slow kitchen sink to a main line that backs up into a floor drain. Water heaters follow, from a pilot that will not stay lit to a leaking tank that has to be replaced the same day.

Then come leaks, toilets, faucets, garbage disposals and the planned work that fills the rest of the schedule, such as repiping, new fixtures and bathroom rough-in. Each one has its own page on the services page, with the signs to look for and the factors that set the price.

How we explain a price

We do not post price lists, because the same symptom can have very different causes. A slow drain might be hair in a trap or roots in a main line. A dead water heater might need an element or a new tank.

What we do instead is explain what sets the price on each job: access, parts, diagnosis time, the pipe material and the hour of the call. The plumber describes the scope and the cost before starting, and you decide whether to go ahead. If you want to compare, ask for the scope in writing.

Permits and code in Puyallup

Some plumbing work needs a permit, such as moving gas lines, replacing a water heater in some cases, or changing drain and vent piping. The City of Puyallup handles permits for addresses inside the city, and its permit portal is at permits.puyallupwa.gov. City Hall is at 333 S. Meridian.

Addresses outside the city limits, including parts of South Hill, fall under Pierce County. When you are not sure which applies, give the dispatcher your address and the cross street, and ask the permit office before work begins.

Where we work

We serve all five Puyallup ZIP codes, 98371 through 98375, plus Sumner, Edgewood, Milton, Orting, Graham, Spanaway and Bonney Lake. The service areas page lists them, and the services page lists what we fix.

Why we are based on South Hill

Our office sits on S Meridian in South Hill, the fast-growing area south of downtown Puyallup that grew by more than 140 percent between 1990 and 2000 and still carries a Puyallup mailing address. A base on that corridor puts dispatch close to both the older downtown streets and the newer subdivisions.

Older homes tend to bring old drain lines, galvanized supply pipe and aging water heaters. Newer homes bring builder-grade fixtures and grease or hair clogs. Knowing which is which before a plumber arrives shortens the visit.

Codes and references

Our work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as amended by Washington and the manufacturer's instructions for each product. Washington requires a licensed plumbing contractor for plumbing work done for others under RCW 18.106, and the Department of Labor and Industries lets you check a license at lni.wa.gov/verify.
