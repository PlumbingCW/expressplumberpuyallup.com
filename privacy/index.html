What this policy covers

This policy describes how Puyallup Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our line. The site has no forms, no accounts and no checkout. The only way to contact us is by phone or email.

Calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber. We use that information to schedule and complete your service.

Website data

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and performance. The page embeds maps from Google Maps, and Google may collect data under its own policy when a map loads. We do not sell your personal information.

Email

If you email us at contact@expressplumberpuyallup.com, we use your message only to respond.

Your choices

You can decline to give information on a call, though we may be unable to schedule service without an address. To ask about information we hold, email us or call (253) 275-5116.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
