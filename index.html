What to do before the emergency plumber arrives

  1. 1

    Stop the water

    Close the main valve, usually near the meter, a garage wall or where the line enters the house.

  2. 2

    Make it safe

    Keep power off in wet rooms if you can reach the panel dry, and keep people away from sewage.

  3. 3

    Call the 24/7 line

    Describe the leak or backup and tell us if the valve will not close.

  4. 4

    Document

    Photograph the damage before cleanup for your insurer.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. Our emergency plumber dispatch handles the plumbing side once everyone is safe.

Local

Plumbing in Puyallup, from downtown to South Hill

Puyallup was platted by Ezra Meeker in 1877 and incorporated in 1890, so the blocks near downtown and the Meeker Mansion include some of the area's oldest homes. Older homes often have older drains and supply pipe, which is why we put a camera on a repeat clog before we recommend a fix. The sewer inspection page shows what that looks like.

South Hill is a different story. It is an unincorporated area of about 64,700 people at the 2020 census with a Puyallup mailing address, and its population grew 143.9 percent between 1990 and 2000. Newer homes tend to need grease and hair clog work, water heater swaps and fixture repairs more than pipe replacement. If you are on South Hill, ask Pierce County about permits and your water provider about your meter.

The Washington State Fair draws more than a million visitors each September, and the fairgrounds, S Meridian (SR-161) and the Meridian Place shopping center sit among busy restaurants and rentals where a grease line or a failed water heater needs fast help. Our commercial plumbing page covers after-hours calls for those businesses.

The City of Puyallup's 2024 water quality report says the city draws mostly from Salmon Springs and Maplewood Springs plus several wells, treats its water with chlorine and reports average hardness of 89 ppm. That moderate hardness leaves scale on tankless heaters and fixtures over time, which is why we recommend an annual tankless flush and regular checks of aerators.
